# EVE: Ore to ISK – v1.0 beta

The first public beta of a free macOS companion for EVE Online's industrial players:
**mine → refine → build → sell → haul**, with the numbers worked out for you.

## Highlights
- **Build Planner** – pick what to build; get the ore to mine (fewest m³), the minerals, the components and a priced shopping list.
- **Reprocessing** – for every stack at your refinery: sell as ore, or reprocess and sell the minerals?
- **Manufacturing & Build vs Sell** – real cash profit at all five trade hubs, with fair-price checks that ignore silly listings.
- **Market Orders** – see at a glance which orders are undercut, and how your finished orders did.
- **Haul** – pack trips by ISK per m³, plan gank-aware routes, send waypoints to the game, and get alerts when a route turns red.
- **Finances** – where your ISK came from, net worth over time, and history kept beyond CCP's 30 days.
- Built-in, searchable **Help** (⌘?).

## Install
1. Download **EVE-Ore-to-ISK-1.0-beta.dmg** below and drag the app into Applications.
2. This beta isn't notarised by Apple yet. The first time, macOS blocks it: open it once, click **Done**,
   then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
3. Log in with your EVE character on the welcome screen.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. SHA-256 of the .dmg: `SHA256_HERE`

## Known limits (beta)
- One character at a time (several characters are planned).
- Not notarised or sandboxed yet; the App Store version comes later.
- First launch downloads CCP's static data (a few minutes).

## Privacy
Login on CCP's own page (PKCE, no secret). The app only talks to CCP and zKillboard. Nothing is collected.
