# EVE: Ore to ISK – v1.4 beta

A free macOS companion for EVE Online's industrial players:
**mine → refine → build → sell → haul**, with the numbers worked out for you.

## What's new in 1.4
- **Reprocess or Sell** – a new page for everything that isn't ore: loot, salvage, old modules,
  packaged ships. Sell it as it is or reprocess it? Checks all five hubs, your skills, station tax
  and whether hauling it to your refinery pays.
- **Build Planner: is it worth it?** – Total Cost, Sells For, Profit and Cash profit for the whole
  plan, and where your stock came from (bought, built, mined, looted, already had).
- **Use stock at other stations** – the Build Planner counts what you have elsewhere and tells you
  what to fetch.
- **Industry job slots** – how many manufacturing, science and reaction jobs you can still start.
- **Market Orders** – your price vs the lowest other, what's really left after tax, fees and build
  cost, the fair price and sold per day.
- Smaller things: Ore Reprocessing renamed, blueprint copy runs, row highlighting, faster pages.

See **CHANGELOG.md** for the full list.

## Install
1. Download **EVE-Ore-to-ISK-1.4-beta.dmg** below and drag the app into Applications
   (replace the old one – your login, settings and history are kept).
2. This beta isn't notarised by Apple yet. The first time, macOS blocks it: open it once, click **Done**,
   then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
3. New to the app? Log in with your EVE character on the welcome screen.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. SHA-256 of the .dmg: `SHA256_HERE`

## Known limits (beta)
- One character at a time (several characters are planned next).
- Not notarised or sandboxed yet; the App Store version comes later.
- First launch downloads CCP's static data (a few minutes).

## Privacy
Login on CCP's own page (PKCE, no secret). The app only talks to CCP and zKillboard. Nothing is collected.
