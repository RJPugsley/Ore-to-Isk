A table of all your blueprints:

- **Blueprint**, and **Kind**: **BPO** (original, unlimited runs) or **BPC** (copy, limited runs).
- **ME** (material efficiency) and **TE** (time efficiency): how far it's been researched.
- **Runs**: runs left on a copy.
- **Qty**: how many identical ones you have.
- **Location** and **Container**: where it sits.
- **In Use**: whether a job is using it right now.

Use **Kind** to show originals, copies or both, and **Location** to show one station or structure.

> Right-click a blueprint to jump to Manufacturing, Build vs Sell or Blueprint Copying for it, or to copy its name.
