1. Dock at the station or structure where you reprocess.
2. In the game, open the Reprocessing window, drag in any ore and note the **yield %** shown.
3. In the app, open **Mine → Reprocessing → Settings** (or Build Settings) and pick that place as your **Refinery**. Type in the yield, choose the ore you measured it on, and enter any refinery tax.
4. Do the same with some ice if you mine ice.
5. Back on **Reprocessing**, every stack there shows **Ore Sell Value** and **Minerals Sell Value**. **Best** tells you which is bigger.
6. Click an ore to see the minerals and the tax in detail.

> Both values are what would actually reach your wallet after fees and tax, and prices are checked against the fair price. So the comparison is like for like.
