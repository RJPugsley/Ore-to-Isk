## What's stored, and where

- **Your login** is in your Mac's **Keychain**, for your macOS user account only.
- **Settings** (your hub, refinery, plans and so on) are saved in the app's preferences.
- **Game data and your saved history** are in a folder on your Mac. Open it with **About EVE: Ore to ISK → Show Data Folder in Finder**.
  - `sde.sqlite`: CCP's static game data. It can always be downloaded again.
  - `history.sqlite`: your saved history. **Back this file up** if you care about your records, because CCP can't give the older entries back.

Nothing is stored inside the app itself, so sharing the app never shares your character.

## Who the app talks to

- **CCP**: the EVE login, the official API (ESI) and the static game data.
- **zKillboard**: public kill counts for the Route and Watch List pages.

That's all. There's no tracking, no adverts and no account with the app's maker.
