## Set up

- **From**: a station or structure where you have things. The busiest places are listed first.
- **Sell at**: the hub you're taking it to. This prices everything.
- **Ship**: one of your ships with a cargo hold of 1,000 m³ or more. The hold size includes your skills and fitted expanders. You can also **type a cargo size** yourself.
- **Cargo**: the room for one trip. If your in-game figure is different, type over it.
- **Warn when a trip is worth over**: trips carrying more than this (in millions) are marked red. That's a sign to split the load, or to fly something tougher.

## Tiles

**To Haul** (m³), **Worth at** the hub, number of **Trips**, the **Route** (jumps and how risky it is), and the **Richest Trip**.

## The list

Each item shows **Qty**, **m³**, **Sell Each**, **Worth**, **ISK / m³**, **Sold / Day** and which **Trip** it goes on.

- Trips are packed with the **most ISK per m³ first**, so trip 1 is always the most valuable use of your hold.
- Big stacks are split across trips.
- Untick **Haul** (or right-click → **Leave Behind**) to drop an item from the plan. The app remembers this. **Take It** puts it back.
- Assembled ships fly themselves, so they're left out. Things that are **Too big** for your hold are flagged.

> Hover over **Worth** for what you'd get selling instantly to buy orders, rather than listing.
