Finances has two tabs: **Overview** and **Transactions**. Everything is worked out over the **time frame** picked at the top right. See [History time frame](help:history-range).

## Overview

### Tiles

- **Wallet**: ISK you have now.
- **Net Worth**: your wallet, plus your assets at CCP's average prices (not counting blueprint copies), plus what your sell orders are worth, plus ISK held in your buy orders. Hover over it to see the parts.
- **In / Out / Net**: money received, money spent, and the difference.

### Charts

- **ISK In and Out per Day**. Over long time frames it's shown per month instead.
- **Net Worth Over Time**. The app saves your net worth once a day when you open it, so the line starts on your second day of using the app.

### Where Your Money Came From

Every wallet entry is sorted into plain categories: market trades, sales tax, broker fees, industry job fees, reprocessing tax, bounties, missions, transfers, contracts, insurance, skill books, clones, planetary tax and *other*.

### Activity cards

- **Mining**: what you mined, valued at your material hub's buy orders, with your top ores.
- **Manufacturing**: sales of things you build, minus the materials you bought for them and the job fees.
- **Trading**: everything else you bought and sold.
- **Fees & taxes**: sales tax, broker fees and refinery tax.

## Transactions

Every purchase and sale: **Date, Type** (buy or sell), **Item, Qty, Price Each, Total** (green for money in, red for money out) and **Where**. Search by item or station. The totals sold and bought are at the bottom.

> CCP only keeps 30 days of wallet history. The app saves it on your Mac each time you open it, so your history builds up the longer you use the app.
