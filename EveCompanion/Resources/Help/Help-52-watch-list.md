## Routes

- **Your routes**: ones you added with **Watch** on the Route page. Double-click a name to rename it.
- **Regular routes**: routes you fly often (10 or more trips in 90 days, either way) are **added by themselves**.

Each card shows the jumps, **Ganks 7 d**, haulers ganked this week, **Kills 1 h** and the worst system on the way.

- **Open** shows the route on the Route page, and **Return Trip** opens the way back.
- **Pin** keeps a route even if you stop flying it.
- **Stop Watching** removes it. The app asks first.

## Places

Your home, where you mine and dock, and where your hangars, jobs and orders are. The places you visit most come first. The list opens by itself when one of them turns amber or red. It shows **kills now and over 24 hours, ganks this week**, and your **Visits** (times docked plus days mined there in the last 90 days).

## How trips are counted

EVE keeps no travel history that an app can read. So while the app is open, it checks every 30 seconds where you are. **Undocking in one place and docking somewhere else counts as one trip.**

## Notifications

While the app is running, it sends a Mac notification when a watched route or place **turns red**, or when a **hauler is ganked** there. It checks about every 15 minutes.
