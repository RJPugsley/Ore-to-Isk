EVE: Ore to ISK helps you make ISK in EVE Online from the ground up: **mine ore, turn it into minerals, build things, sell them and haul them safely**. It reads your character through CCP's official API and works out the figures that the game makes you work out by hand.

## The five tabs

Each tab across the top of the window covers one step of the job.

- **Pilot**: your character, skills and money. Start here.
- **Mine**: what you've mined, what to mine next, and whether to reprocess it.
- **Build**: what to build, what it costs, what it earns, and your blueprints and jobs.
- **Sell**: market prices at the five trade hubs, and how your own orders are doing.
- **Haul**: packing your stuff into trips, planning a safe route, and watching for trouble.

Some pages fit more than one step, so they show up in more than one tab. Build vs Sell is in both **Build** and **Sell**, for example. It's the same page with the same settings wherever you open it.

## Moving around

- **Click a tab** to go to it. Each tab remembers the page you last had open.
- **Hover over a tab** to drop down a list of its pages, then click one.
- **⌘1 to ⌘5** switch tabs: ⌘1 is Pilot and ⌘5 is Haul.
- The page name is shown as the window title.
- **Right-click almost anything** (an item, a station, a system) for shortcuts to other pages and to the game. See [Right-click menus](help:right-click-menus).

> The first time you open the app, a welcome screen walks you through logging in and your setup. New here? Log in first ([Logging in](help:logging-in)), then set where you build and refine in [Build settings](help:build-settings). Every figure in the app depends on those two things.

## What the app never does

- It never changes anything in your game except when you ask it to: setting a destination, adding waypoints or opening a window in the game.
- It never sends your data anywhere except to CCP (and zKillboard for kill counts).
- It never costs anything. It's free, made under CCP's developer licence.
