## Job slots (very top)

A tile for each kind of slot:

- **Manufacturing** – building things.
- **Science** – research (ME and TE), copying and invention all share these slots.
- **Reactions** – only shown once you have Mass Reactions or a reaction running.

Each tile says how many slots are in use out of how many you have, and how many more jobs you can start. It turns **orange** when every slot is taken. Hover over a tile to see which skills give you the slots.

> A finished job keeps its slot until you deliver it, so deliver **Ready** jobs to free the slot up.

## Active jobs

Each running job with its product, runs, a progress bar, the time left (or **Ready**, when it's waiting for you to deliver it), where it is and what it cost. **No Active Jobs** means every slot is free.

## Completed (bottom)

Jobs that finished within the time frame: **Finished, Item, Activity, Runs, Fee, Where** and **Status**. Above the table is a line for this month, e.g. *120 runs built in 8 jobs · 1.2m fees*.

> Finished jobs are saved each time you open the app, so this list builds up over time.
