Almost everything in the app can be right-clicked.

## On an item

- **Show in Market**: opens it on the [Market](help:market) page.
- If it can be built: **Build vs Sell**, **Manufacturing**, **Add to Build Planner** and **Blueprint Payback**.
- **In EVE**: **Show Market Details in Game** or **Show Info in Game**. The window opens inside your game client.
- **Add to / Remove from Market Watchlist**.
- **Copy Name**, and **Copy for Multibuy**: paste it into the game's Multibuy window to buy it all in one go.
- **EVE Ref**: the item's page on the EVE Ref website.

## On a station, structure or system

- **Route from Here**, **Route to Here**, **Add as a Stop on the Route**, **Avoid** the system.
- **In EVE**: **Set Destination in Game**, **Add Waypoint in Game**, **Show Info in Game**.
- **Copy Name**, **Dotlan** (maps) and **zKillboard** (recent kills).

## Materials lists

On Build vs Sell, Blueprint Payback, Blueprint Copying and Blueprints, right-clicking a blueprint lists its **materials** at your ME. **Copy for Multibuy** copies the whole shopping list.

> Actions in the game need one extra permission. If they don't work, log out and in again once. See [Logging in](help:logging-in).

## Deleting

Anything that removes something (a plan, a watched route) **asks first**. Tick **Don't ask again** to skip the question next time. **Settings → Ask Before Deleting Again** turns all the questions back on.
