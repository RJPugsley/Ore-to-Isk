The app needs to log in to your character so it can read your skills, wallet, assets, orders, blueprints and industry jobs. You log in on CCP's own website. The app never sees your EVE password.

## Logging in

1. Choose **Character → Log In with EVE Online…** from the menu bar. Most pages also show a **Log In with EVE Online** button while you're logged out.
2. A secure login window opens with CCP's own login page. If you're already logged in to the EVE website in Safari, you only need to pick the character.
3. CCP lists what the app is asking to read. Click **Authorize**.
4. The window closes by itself and the app loads your character.

The dot at the top left of the window turns **green** and says **Connected** once you're in.

## Where the login is kept

Your login is stored in your Mac's **Keychain**, under your own macOS user account. It is not inside the app. If you give the app to a friend, they log in with their own character and never see yours.

## "Needs permission"

Sometimes a page or the character summary says **Needs permission**. A new feature needs something your login didn't approve yet, such as opening windows in the game or setting waypoints. To fix it:

1. Open **Settings** (⌘,) and click **Log Out**.
2. Choose **Character → Log In with EVE Online…** again and approve the new list.

You only need to do this once for each new permission.

## Logging out

**Settings (⌘,) → Log Out** removes the login from your Keychain. Your saved history and settings stay on your Mac, ready for the next time you log in.

> Logging in again with a different character replaces the current one. Support for several characters at once is planned.
