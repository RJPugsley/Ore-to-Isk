Ore, compressed ore and ice aren't here – they're on [Ore Reprocessing](help:reprocessing). Assembled ships are left out; repackage one to include it.

This page goes through everything you own that reprocesses into something – loot, salvage, old modules, packaged ships, empty containers – and tells you, for each stack, whether it's worth more **sold as it is** or **reprocessed**.

Some things break down more than once: an item gives salvage or parts, and those reprocess again into minerals. The page follows every piece all the way down and, at each step, picks whichever pays more – selling that piece or reprocessing it again. So the **Reprocessed** figure is always the best you could do with what comes out.

## What's left out

- **Assembled ships** – and anything fitted to them or sitting in their cargo. Repackage a ship to include it.
- Blueprints, skills and implants in your head.
- Anything that doesn't reprocess into anything.

## The controls

- **My Stuff / Look Up**: your own items, or any item you type in.
- **Where**: everywhere, or one station.
- **Reprocess at**:
  - **Where it is** – at the station the stack is in.
  - **My refinery** – at the refinery from [Build settings](help:build-settings). Hover the station to see how many m³ and jumps it is from there.
  - **Compare both** – whichever pays more for each item. **Reprocess at refinery** in the Verdict means it pays to take it there first.
- **Sell at**: one hub, or **Best hub**. Each item is always priced at one hub only – the item and everything it breaks down into – so you never have to haul the pieces to different places.
- **Prices**: **Sell now** uses what buyers pay today; **List and wait** uses what sellers ask, less the broker fee.

## The table

- **As Is**: cash from selling the stack as it is, after selling fees.
- **Reprocessed**: cash from reprocessing it and doing the best thing with each piece, after reprocessing tax and selling fees. **Need 100** (or similar) means the stack is smaller than one reprocessing lot; **Can't here** means the station has no reprocessing plant. Orange means someone else's structure, where the yield and tax can't be read and are guessed.
- **Verdict**: **Reprocess**, **Sell as is**, or **Either** when the two are within 5%.
- **Gain**: how much more (or less) reprocessing brings.
- **Hub** and **Sold / Day**: where it's priced, and how many sell per day there. Orange means fewer sell per day than you have.

Right-click a row for the item and place menus. Click a column heading to sort.

## One item in detail

Click a row to see:

- **All Five Hubs** – what it's worth as it is and reprocessed at each hub. If selling the pieces at different hubs would pay noticeably more, a note says so.
- **How it breaks down** – every step, with what each piece is worth as it is and reprocessed. The bold figure is the one that counts. Click the arrows to open or close a step.

## Why reprocessing often loses

Modules, salvage and other non-ore items come back at about half – the station's equipment rate (50% almost everywhere), raised a little by the **Scrapmetal Processing** skill. Refinery rigs, implants and the ore skills don't help them. Each extra step loses about half again, which is why breaking things down further often doesn't pay.

NPC stations also take a reprocessing tax that falls as your standing with the station's owner rises, and is gone at 6.67. Your refinery uses the tax you entered in Build settings.

> Prices are checked against the fair price first, so one silly order on the market won't fool the page. See [Price checks & badges](help:price-checks).
