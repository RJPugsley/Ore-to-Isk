Pages that show results over a period have a **time frame picker** at the top right: **30 Days**, **90 Days**, **1 Year** (the default) or **All**.

There's only **one** setting. Change it on Finances and the Mining Ledger, Market Orders results, Industry Jobs, Manufacturing's track record, Build vs Sell and Blueprint Copying all follow.

## Where the history comes from

CCP only lets apps see about **30 days** of your wallet and a limited stretch of your mining ledger. So each time you open or refresh the app, it **saves a copy on your Mac**:

- wallet entries and transactions
- your mining ledger
- your market orders and how they finished
- industry jobs, including finished ones
- your wallet and net worth, once a day
- snapshots of the competition for the items you build, sell or watch

The longer you use the app, the further back your history goes. If you don't open the app for more than about a month, there may be a gap.

> Detailed market snapshots are thinned out as they get older: every few minutes for a week, then hourly for 90 days, then daily for a year. That keeps the file small.
