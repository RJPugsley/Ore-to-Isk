The big question for a builder: if you put minerals into this blueprint, do you earn more than you'd get by simply **selling those minerals**? This page answers it for a whole group of blueprints at once.

## Controls

- **Search**, **Category** and **Group** pick which blueprints to list. Counts show how many are in each.
- **Runs**: every figure is for this many runs. Prices are averaged over the orders it would take to sell that many, so big batches aren't flattered by one cheap order.
- **Sell at**: one hub, or **Best hub** (whichever gives the most).
- **Materials valued at**: the hub whose buy orders decide what the materials are worth if sold.
- **ME if not owned**: the research level to assume for blueprints you don't have.
- **Only My Blueprints**.
- **Refresh Prices**. The page also reloads itself every 5 minutes.

## Columns

- **Item**: with ×units per run. Hover for its group, tech level and materials.
- **Blueprint**: yours (original or copy, with ME) or *Not owned*.
- **Materials**: what the materials would put in your wallet if you sold them instead.
- **Sale**: what the product would bring in, after fees, and at which hub.
- **Verdict**: e.g. **Build +38%** (building earns 38% more than selling the materials), **Sell mats −15%**, or **Either** when it's within 5%.
- **Gain / Hour** (hidden by default): profit per hour of build time.
- **Fair Price**: the believable price from recent sales. Badges warn of odd prices.
- **Listed · Sold/Day**: how much is on the market and how fast it sells, with ▲/▼ for more or fewer sellers than a week ago.

## Watching items

Click **☆** next to an item to watch it. The app then records how its competition changes over time, which shows up as ▲/▼ here and on Manufacturing.

> Hover over the **?** button for a key to every column and badge.
