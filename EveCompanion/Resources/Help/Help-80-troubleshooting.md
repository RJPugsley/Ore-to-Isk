### A page says "Not Logged In" or "Needs permission"

Log in with **Character → Log In with EVE Online…**. For *Needs permission*, log out in **Settings (⌘,)** and log in again so you can approve the new permission. See [Logging in](help:logging-in).

### "Preparing Game Data" won't go away

The first download of CCP's game data takes a few minutes. If it fails, check your internet connection and choose **Try Again**.

### Pages say "Couldn't Load…" around 11:00 UTC

That's EVE's **daily downtime**. The game's servers restart at about 11:00 EVE time (UTC) for around half an hour. The app shows its last figures and carries on afterwards.

### "Some prices couldn't be fetched – EVE's servers timed out"

CCP's servers were busy. Click **Refresh Prices** in a minute.

### My mineral or yield numbers are slightly different from the game

The game rounds the yield % it shows you, so totals can be off by about 0.05%. Make sure your refinery and yields are set in [Build settings](help:build-settings). Remember to set the ice yield separately.

### Reprocessing or Haul Planner says there's nothing there

They only look at **one place**: your chosen refinery, or the *From* station. Check you've picked the right one, and press **Refresh**. Your assets are re-read about every 10 minutes.

### Profit looks too good to be true

Look for an **Inflated** badge, a high **Market Days** figure or a low **Sold / Day**. The app already uses the fair price when a listing is inflated, but a market that only sells one a week won't take ten. See [Price checks & badges](help:price-checks).

### My history only goes back a month

CCP only gives apps about 30 days. The app saves everything from the day you start using it, so your history grows from then on. See [History time frame](help:history-range).

### Route search only finds systems, not stations

Finding player structures needs an extra permission. Log out and in again to approve it.

### Waypoints or "Show in Game" do nothing

Your game client needs to be running and logged in as the same character. The first time, log out and in again in the app to approve the permission for game windows.

### Net Worth Over Time is empty

Net worth is saved once a day when you open the app. The line appears from your second day.

### I don't get Watch List notifications

Notifications only come while the app is running. Check that macOS allows notifications for EVE: Ore to ISK in **System Settings → Notifications**.
