Here's the full loop the app is built around. The example builds **10 Ventures** (a mining frigate), but it works the same for anything.

## 1. Decide what to build

Open **Build → Build vs Sell**, pick **Ship → Frigate** and set **Runs** to 10. Look for a **Build +%** verdict, a believable **Fair Price** (no *Inflated* badge) and a decent **Sold/Day**. Building should pay better than selling the minerals, and the market should be able to take what you make.

## 2. Plan the mining

Right-click your pick and choose **Add to Build Planner**. Set the runs to 10 and tick where you mine (e.g. *High*). The planner lists the **ore to mine**, in whichever grade your belts have, and anything you'll need to buy.

## 3. Mine and refine

Mine the ore and take it to your refinery. Open **Mine → Reprocessing** to check whether you should reprocess. For ore you're building with, you will. Reprocess it in the game.

## 4. Check the numbers and build

Open **Build → Manufacturing** and pick the blueprint. **Materials** shows what you have, what's still missing and what that costs. **Cash Profit** shows what you'll make at each hub. Start the job in the game, and follow it on **Build → Industry Jobs**.

## 5. Haul it to market

When the job's done, open **Haul → Haul Planner** from your build station to your sell hub. Then use **Haul → Route** to check the way is green and send the route to your game.

## 6. Sell and keep an eye on it

List the ships at the hub in the game. **Sell → Market Orders** tells you when you've been undercut. Right-click → **Update Price – Open Market in Game** to fix it.

## 7. See how you really did

**Pilot → Finances** and the **Your Track Record** section on Manufacturing show your **actual** profit, worked out from what you really paid and sold for.
