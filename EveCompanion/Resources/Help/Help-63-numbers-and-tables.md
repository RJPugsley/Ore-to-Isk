## Short numbers

To keep tables tidy, numbers are shortened to **at most 5 digits and a letter**:

- **k** = thousand: *12.5k* is 12,500
- **m** = million: *4.60m* is 4,600,000
- **b** = billion: *1.23b* is 1,230,000,000

There's no "ISK" after amounts, since nearly everything is ISK. **Hover over a number** to see the exact figure.

## Hover for details

Almost every figure has a tooltip that appears after a moment. It explains where the number comes from: which orders, which skills, which fees.

## Tables

- **Click a column heading** to sort. Click it again to reverse the order.
- Most pages have a **?** button that explains every column and badge on that page.
- Search boxes **suggest matches as you type**. Use the arrow keys and Return to pick one.

## Colours

- **Green**: good, best, or on top.
- **Orange**: worth a look: a fallback price, an old figure, beaten, or expiring soon.
- **Red**: a loss, or danger.
- **Purple**: a dumped price.
