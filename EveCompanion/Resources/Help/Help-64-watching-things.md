## Market Watchlist (★): items

Star an item to keep it handy in the **★ Watchlist** on the [Market](help:market) page. Right-click any item and choose **Add to Market Watchlist**, or click **☆** on Build vs Sell.

Watched items also get their **competition recorded**: how many sellers and units are listed over time. That's what drives the ▲ / ▼ arrows on Build vs Sell and Manufacturing. The app also tracks these by itself:

- products of blueprints you own
- items you have orders for

## Watch List (Haul tab): routes and places

The [Watch List](help:watch-list) watches **routes and places** for danger: kills, ganks and red lights. It can send you a Mac notification.
