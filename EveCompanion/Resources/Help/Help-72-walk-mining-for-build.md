1. Open **Mine → Build Planner**.
2. Add what you want to build and set the runs. You can add several items. The planner adds them all together.
3. Tick **where you mine**. Use *High* only if you stay in high-sec.
4. Leave **Subtract what I already have** ticked, so minerals in your hangar and ore at your refinery are used first.
5. Read **Ore to Mine**. Pick *one* column per ore, matching the grade you find in the belt.
6. Check **Everything Else** for parts that don't come from ore, such as planetary or moon materials, and buy those.
7. As you mine, refresh the page: your stock goes up, and the amount left to mine goes down.

> Hover over an ore row to see the total m³ for each grade. Handy for knowing how many hold-loads it is.
