## Settings (⌘,)

- **EVE Online Login**: who's logged in, **Log Out**, and **Log In with EVE Online…**
- **Confirmations**: **Ask Before Deleting Again** brings back every *Are you sure?* question you turned off with *Don't ask again*.
- **Start Fresh…**: logs out, forgets every setting and puts the app's data aside, then quits. The next launch is like a brand-new download: the welcome screen, the game data download and the login. Your old data is kept in a folder next to the new one, in case you want it back.

## About (app menu → About EVE: Ore to ISK)

The version number, **Show Data Folder in Finder**, and the notices about CCP and data credits.
