## Left side: finding an item

- **Search**: start typing and pick from the suggestions.
- **Market**: the full market tree, the same groups as in the game. Click to open groups, then pick an item.
- **★ Watchlist**: the items you've starred, shown in their market groups.

Search results and the Watchlist are laid out in their own market groups, so you can always see what kind of item something is.

## Right side: the item

- **Tiles**: the prices, the gap between buy and sell, how many sell per day, and how much competition there is.
- **Fair Price**: what recent sales say it's really worth. See [Price checks & badges](help:price-checks).
- **The Five Hubs**: **Lowest Sell** and **Listed** (units for sale), **Highest Buy** and **Wanted** (units people want to buy) at Jita, Amarr, Dodixie, Rens and Hek. **Green** marks the cheapest place to buy and the best place to sell straight away.
- **Price History** (30 days, 90 days, 6 months or 1 year): the daily average price as a line, the daily low to high as a band, and units sold as bars. Two dashed lines show the **Period average** (orange) and the **Fair price** (green).
- **Sellers** and **Buyers**: every order, with **Price, Quantity, Location, Security** and **Expires**. Buy orders also show **Min. Volume** and **Range**.

**Orders in** switches between the whole region around a hub and just the five hub stations.

> Right-click an item, anywhere in the app, and choose **Add to Market Watchlist** to star it.
