1. Open **Build → Blueprint Payback** and tick **Hide ones I own**.
2. Pick a **Category** and **Group** you can build, e.g. *Ship → Frigate*.
3. Look at the top of the list: few **Runs to Pay Back** (green), and a healthy **Sold / Day**.
4. Check **Market Days**. If the market needs weeks to absorb the pay-back runs, payback will take longer than it looks.
5. Right-click a promising one and choose **Show in Market**. Check the price history for a steady price rather than a one-off spike.
6. Compare it on **Build vs Sell** with what you already build.

> **Never +x** means a run loses money at today's prices. The arrow shows whether the price is heading the right way.
