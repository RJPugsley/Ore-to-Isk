## Choosing what to build

The list on the left shows **My Blueprints**. The search box finds **anything that can be manufactured**, even if you don't own the blueprint. In that case the page says **You don't own this blueprint** and uses a typical ME/TE.

Set the **Runs** at the top. **ME** and **TE** come from your blueprint.

## Tiles

- **Build Time**: how long the job takes, with your skills and facility.
- **Job Fee**: what starting the job costs.
- **Materials (to buy)**: what you still have to buy.
- **Out of Pocket**: the cash you need up front.
- **Best Hub**: where you'd make the most cash profit.

## Selling at each trade hub

One row for each of the five hubs:

- **Price Each**: what you'd list it for.
- **Cash Profit**: the sale, minus broker fee and sales tax, minus the job fee and anything you still have to buy. Materials you already own count as free.
- **Profit Each**, **Margin** (profit as a share of the price) and **ISK / Hour** (profit for each hour of build time).
- **Competition**: units already listed for sale there, with ▲ / ▼ if there are more or fewer sellers than a week ago.

## Materials

For each material: **Needed, In Stock** (at your stock location, including containers), **From Ore** (what your ore at the refinery would give), **To Buy, Unit Price, Value** and **Buy Cost**.

## Breakdown

Open **How this was calculated** to see the details behind the job fee and taxes: base time, item value, system cost index, facility tax, the SCC surcharge, and your sales tax with your Accounting level.

## Your Track Record

Under the calculator, the app shows how this item has *actually* done for you over the time frame. It lists how many you built, the **real cost each**, what they **sold for each**, the **actual profit each** compared with the calculator, and how many are still in stock. Ore, minerals and stock you already had count as free. Bought materials count at what you paid.

> **Recalculate** fetches fresh prices. **Build Settings** opens the settings panel on the right.
