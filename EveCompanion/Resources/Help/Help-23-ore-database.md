A quick reference for every ore in the game (compressed versions aren't listed).

## Columns

- **Ore**: hover to see its family and how many units make one batch.
- **Yield**: your yield at your refinery with your skills.
- **Most Produced, Second, Third**: its main minerals, with the amounts per batch.
- **Also**: anything else it gives. Only ice, Spodumain and a few moon ores give more than three.
- **Found In**: the safest security where it turns up, e.g. *0.9 · High*, coloured like the game.

## My Yield

- **On**: amounts are what *you* would get at your refinery with your skills.
- **Off**: the game's base amounts, as in the game's Industry window.

## Search

Type an ore, a family or a mineral. For example, *Mexallon* lists every ore that gives Mexallon, with the matches highlighted.

> Right-click an ore and choose **Plan Mining in the Build Planner** to start a plan.

> Open it in its own window with **⇧⌘O** (Window → Ore Database) and keep it beside the main window while you plan.
