## Top strip

- **Training**: the skill training right now, its level boxes, a progress bar and the time left. It turns orange when your queue is empty: **Nothing training – your queue is empty**.
- **Training Queue**: the total time left in the queue, how many skills it holds and when it ends.
- **Total Skill Points**, plus any **unallocated** points waiting to be spent.

## Skill groups

The grid shows all skill groups (Industry, Resource Processing, Trade and so on). Each tile shows how many of that group's skills you've trained. The coloured fill shows **how much of the group you've trained**. Click a group to list its skills below. The app remembers your choice.

## Skill list

Each skill shows five level boxes:

- **filled**: trained
- **coloured**: in your training queue
- **dotted**: not trained yet

On the right you'll see the time left if it's queued, **✓** once it's at level V, or **Not trained**. Hover over a skill to see its skill points.

**Search** finds matching skills across all groups at once.

> Right-click a skill to **Show Info in Game** or copy its name.
