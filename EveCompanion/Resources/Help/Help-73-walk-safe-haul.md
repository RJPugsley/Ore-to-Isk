1. **Pack sensibly.** On **Haul → Haul Planner**, set **Warn when a trip is worth over** to an amount you're willing to lose. Red trips are tempting targets, so split them or use a tougher ship.
2. **Check the way.** On **Haul → Route**, set the **Autopilot** panel to match your game, then choose **Lowest Risk**. Read the **Why** next to any amber or red system.
3. **Avoid hot spots.** Right-click a red system and choose **Avoid**. The route goes around it.
4. **Send it.** Click **Send route**. The app adds waypoints only where it needs to, to keep you on the safe path.
5. **Watch it.** Click **Watch** to add the route to the **Watch List**. If it turns red, or a hauler is ganked on it, you'll get a notification while the app is running.

> Gank hot spots change all the time. Check again just before you undock, especially with a valuable load.
