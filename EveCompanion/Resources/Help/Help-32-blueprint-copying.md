Players sell blueprint copies (BPCs) on public contracts. This page reads those contracts in a hub's region and works out whether copying is worth your lab time.

## Settings

- **Sell copies in**: the hub whose region's contracts are checked.
- **Runs per copy**: *0* means the most runs a copy of that blueprint can have.
- **Copies**: how many copies you'd make.
- **Search**, **Category**, **Group** and **Only My Blueprints** narrow the list.

## Columns

- **Copy Time / Run**: how long copying takes with your Science and Advanced Industry skills.
- **Fee / Run**: what the copy job costs.
- **Price / Run**: what copies sell for on contracts.
- **Margin / Run** and **Verdict**: whether copying pays.
- **Contracts**: how many offers there are. Hover to see recent ones.

## Copies sold

The app keeps a record of copy contracts. One that disappears before it expires was **probably sold**. That gives you an idea of what really sells, not just what's listed. The record is checked every 30 minutes while the app is open.

> The first check of a region reads all its public contracts and takes a minute or two. After that only new contracts are read.
