## Tiles

- **Units** and **m³** mined over the time frame.
- **Value**: worth at your material hub's buy orders (or CCP's average price if there are none). This isn't cash until you sell it.
- **Days mined**.

## Chart

- **Group by**: *Day*, *Week* or *Month*. Week and month mean the last **7 days** and **30 days**, counted back from today. They aren't calendar weeks or months, so the latest bar is always a full one.
- **Show**: *ISK*, *m³* or *Units*.

## Tables

All three tables can be sorted by clicking a column heading.

- **By Ore**: each ore with units, m³, value, its price each and its share of the total.
- **By System**: where you mined, with the system's security.
- **Ledger**: every day's entries.

> The game only keeps your mining ledger for a limited time. The app saves it each time you open it, so your record grows from the day you start using the app.
