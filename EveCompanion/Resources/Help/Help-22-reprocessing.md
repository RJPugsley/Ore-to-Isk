This page looks at the ore and ice you have at your refinery. For each stack, it compares **selling it as it is** with **reprocessing it and selling the minerals**.

First pick your refinery in [Build settings](help:build-settings). If you haven't, the page shows **Choose Your Refinery**.

## The ore table

- **Ore**, **Quantity** and **Yield**: your yield for that ore with your skills. Hover to see which skill it uses.
- **Reprocesses Into**: the minerals you'd get.
- **Ore Sell Value**: the cash you'd end up with selling the ore at your hub, after selling fees.
- **Minerals Sell Value**: the cash you'd end up with reprocessing and selling the minerals, after refinery tax and selling fees.
- **Best**: **Reprocess** or **Sell Ore**, with the better value in green. **Need 100 to reprocess** (or similar) means the stack is smaller than one batch.

Tick **Hide stacks too small to reprocess** to hide those. Click a column heading to sort.

## One ore in detail

Click an ore to see exactly what it turns into. The tiles show **Sell as Ore**, **Reprocess & Sell Minerals** and how much more one way gains. The table shows each mineral's **Quantity, Unit Price, Value, Reprocessing Cost** (the refinery's tax) and **Net**. **Show All Ore** goes back to the list.

With nothing selected, **Minerals From All Ore Combined** shows the total from reprocessing everything.

> Prices are checked against the fair price first, so one silly order on the market won't fool the page. See [Price checks & badges](help:price-checks).
