This is the first page you see. Its title is your character's name.

## What it shows

- **Portrait, name, corporation and alliance.**
- **Wallet**: the ISK you have now. Under it is how much your **net worth** has grown or shrunk over the time frame picked at the top right (for example *+12% · 90 Days*). Net worth is your wallet plus everything you own; see [Finances](help:finances).
- **Home station**: where you wake up in a new clone.
- **Playing for**: how long the character has existed, e.g. *4 years, 2 months*.
- **Security status**: green when positive, red when negative.
- **Corporation** (and **Alliance**, if you're in one).

### Right Now

- **Location**: the system you're in, with its security (green for high-sec, orange for low-sec, red for null), its region, and either **Docked at …** or **In space**.
- **Ship**: the ship you're sitting in, with its name and type.

The page refreshes itself every minute, so it follows you as you fly.

> Right-click your location or ship for quick actions, such as planning a route from here or showing the item in the market.
