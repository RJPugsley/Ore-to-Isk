## Tiles

- **Sell Orders**: how many you have, and the value still listed.
- **Buy Orders**: how many you have, and the ISK held in escrow.
- **Undercut / Outbid**: how many orders someone has beaten. It shows **All Orders on Top** when that's none.
- **Order Slots**: used out of your maximum, which your Trade skills decide.

## Sell and Buy tabs

- **Status symbol**: an orange warning triangle means you've been **undercut** (sell) or **outbid** (buy). A green tick means you're the cheapest or highest, or have the only order. Hover for the words.
- **Item, Station**.
- **Yours / Lowest Other** (or **Highest Other** for buy orders): your price, then the best competing price at the same station, not counting yours. The percentage shows how far the other price is from yours: green when you're ahead, orange when you've been beaten. For example, *500.00k / 499.90k (−0.02%)* in orange means someone sells 0.02% cheaper. Hover for exact prices and how many units compete.
  A small orange **down-arrow** next to the lowest price means that seller is well below the fair price, probably a one-off dump. It may pay to wait rather than match it.
- **Fair Price**: what the item normally sells for in the region lately. It's the middle of recent sales and follows a real trend, the same green line as on the [Market](help:market) chart. An arrow shows a trend up or down. The percentage is your price against it: orange when you're more than 10% above it (it'll sit), green otherwise.
- **Sold / Day**: how many sell per day across the region, all sellers together. Hover to see how many days of sales your remaining units are.
- **Remaining** (e.g. *40 / 100*).
- **Net Left** (sell orders): what the rest of the order is really worth to you. It starts from units left × your price, then takes off the **sales tax** you'll pay when it sells, this order's share of the **broker fees** (the fee when you listed it, plus a relist fee for every price change the app saw), and what the items **cost you**. Built items count at what the build cost you, bought items at what you paid, and mined or looted items cost nothing. Hover over the figure to see the whole sum. It turns red if the order now loses money. For buy orders this column shows the **Escrow** instead.
- **Expires**. It turns orange under 3 days.

The **Sell Orders** tile at the top shows the total listed and, after it, the total net.

Broker fees at player structures are set by the owner, and EVE doesn't share that figure, so the app estimates them there and says so.

Beaten orders are listed first. Competitors' prices are re-checked every 5 minutes while the page is open.

> Right-click a beaten order and choose **Update Price – Open Market in Game**. The game opens that item's market window so you can change your price.

## Results tab

Orders that **filled, expired or were cancelled** within the time frame:

- **Outcome, Side, Item, Station, Your Price**.
- **Traded**: units sold or bought out of the total.
- **Got Each**: the average price you actually got.
- **Took**: how long it took to fill.

The tiles show how many finished, the typical **time to fill**, **got vs listed**, and how often you were undercut.
