### ISK
EVE's money (InterStellar Kredits).

### m³ (cubic metres)
How much room something takes in a cargo hold. A hold's size is also measured in m³.

### Trade hub
A station where most of a region's trading happens. The app compares the five big ones: **Jita, Amarr, Dodixie, Rens and Hek**.

### Region
A group of star systems. Buy orders can reach any station in a region, and price history is kept per region.

### Sell order / buy order
A **sell order** is something listed for sale. A **buy order** is someone offering to buy. Selling instantly means selling to the best buy order. Listing means placing your own sell order and waiting.

### Spread
The gap between the cheapest sell order and the best buy order.

### Undercut / outbid
Someone listed the same item cheaper than your sell order (undercut), or offered more than your buy order (outbid).

### Escrow
The ISK set aside to pay for your buy orders.

### Broker fee
Charged when you place an order at a market. Your Broker Relations skill and your standings make it smaller.

### Sales tax
Charged when something sells. The Accounting skill makes it smaller.

### Blueprint (BPO / BPC)
The recipe for building something. A **BPO** (original) can be used for ever. A **BPC** (copy) has a set number of **runs**.

### Run
One go of a blueprint. One run usually makes one item, but ammunition and some other items make many.

### ME (material efficiency)
Research on a blueprint that cuts the materials it needs, up to 10%.

### TE (time efficiency)
Research on a blueprint that cuts the build time, up to 20%.

### T1 / T2
Tech 1 items are the basic versions. Tech 2 items are better, need blueprints made by **invention**, and use extra materials.

### Job fee and cost index
What starting an industry job costs. It goes up in systems where a lot of industry happens (a high **cost index**). It includes the facility's tax and CCP's **SCC surcharge**.

### Estimated item value (EIV)
A value CCP gives each item. The job fee is worked out from it.

### Adjusted / average price
CCP's own reference prices for every item. The app uses them when the market has no useful orders.

### Reprocessing (refining)
Breaking ore (or items) down into minerals at a station or structure.

### Yield
The share of an ore's minerals you actually get when reprocessing. It depends on the refinery and your skills.

### Portion / batch
Ore reprocesses in fixed amounts, usually 100 units. Anything left over stays as ore.

### Ore grades (Standard, II-Grade, III-Grade)
Better grades of the same ore give more minerals per unit.

### Compression
Squeezing ore into a much smaller form, which makes it easier to haul. It gives the same minerals.

### High-sec / low-sec / null-sec
How safe a system is. **High-sec** (0.5 to 1.0) is policed by CONCORD. **Low-sec** (0.1 to 0.4) and **null-sec** (0.0 and below) are not.

### Security status
For a system, how safe it is. For a character, their standing with the law. Negative status can stop you entering high-sec freely.

### CONCORD
EVE's police in high-sec. They destroy anyone who attacks without permission, but only *after* the attack.

### Gank
Attackers in high-sec destroy a ship knowing CONCORD will kill them afterwards. They do it because the loot is worth more than their losses. Haulers carrying expensive cargo are common targets.

### Multibuy
A window in the game for buying a whole shopping list at once. **Copy for Multibuy** fills it for you.

### Downtime
EVE's servers restart every day at about 11:00 UTC for around half an hour.

### ESI
CCP's official interface that lets apps read game data, with your permission.

### SDE (static data)
CCP's file of everything that doesn't change between patches: items, blueprints, ores and systems.

### zKillboard
A public website that records ship kills. The app uses it to count kills and ganks.

### Hauler
A ship built to carry cargo, or the pilot flying it.
